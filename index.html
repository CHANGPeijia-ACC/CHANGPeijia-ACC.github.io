Singapore Government Procurement (GeBIZ) — Audit Analysis新加坡政府采购（GeBIZ）审计式分析

github.com/CHANGPeijia-ACC/sg-procurement-analysis

Where would a procurement audit be worth looking? An analysis of 18,464 public award records to find out.

一次采购审计该从哪里入手？对 18,464 条公开授标记录的分析。

Problem
Procurement audit scoping is usually driven by judgement and prior year. Singapore publishes every government award — so the question is whether that public record can point to where a closer look is justified, before anyone opens a file.
采购审计的范围划定通常依赖经验和上年底稿。新加坡公开披露每一笔政府授标，问题就变成：在打开任何一份档案之前，这份公开记录能否指出值得细看的地方。
Approach
Python and pandas over 18,464 award records across 113 agencies and 6,152 supplier names (FY2021–FY2025, data.gov.sg). Three tests: amount-weighted HHI and CR4 concentration per agency; award clustering in shrinking windows (±10% / ±5% / ±2%) around the published S$6,000 and S$90,000 procurement thresholds; and a Benford chi-square test on leading digits at several minimum-amount floors. Supplier-name normalisation was kept deliberately conservative — punctuation and legal-suffix spelling only, no fuzzy matching — and every one of the 7 merged groups was verified by hand.
用 Python 与 pandas 处理 18,464 条授标记录，覆盖 113 个机构、6,152 个供应商名称（FY2021–FY2025，data.gov.sg）。做三项检验：按机构的金额加权 HHI 与 CR4 集中度；围绕 6,000 与 90,000 新元采购门槛、以逐步收窄的窗口（±10% / ±5% / ±2%）统计授标聚集；以及在多个最小金额下限上对首位数字做 Benford 卡方检验。供应商名称规范化刻意保守——只统一标点与法定后缀拼写，不做模糊匹配——合并的 7 组全部人工复核。
Findings
Award amounts cluster below the S$90,000 open-tender threshold, and the below:above ratio sharpens as the window narrows (1.25x at ±10%, 1.61x at ±2%) — the signature of deliberate clustering rather than noise. Several agencies show HHI above 4,000 despite having dozens or hundreds of suppliers on record, because one or two contracts dominate dollar spend. Benford's law fails on the full dataset (p < 0.0001) but fits closely once sub-S$1,000 items are excluded (p = 0.12), which says more about the test's assumptions than about the data.
授标金额在 9 万新元公开招标门槛下方聚集，且窗口越窄、下方对上方的比值越高（±10% 时 1.25 倍，±2% 时 1.61 倍），这是刻意聚集而非随机噪声的特征。多个机构在拥有数十甚至数百家供应商的情况下 HHI 仍超过 4,000，因为一两份大合同主导了金额。Benford 检验在全样本下不通过（p < 0.0001），但剔除 1,000 新元以下的小额记录后拟合良好（p = 0.12）——这说明的是检验本身的适用前提，而非数据异常。

Limitations

Threshold clustering and Benford deviation are screening signals, not evidence — both have innocent explanations. The concentration figures are a lower bound, since conservative name matching never merges distinct entities but may miss true duplicates. Whole-of-government metrics are not meaningful; every number should be read at agency level.

门槛聚集与 Benford 偏离是筛查信号，不是证据，二者都存在合理解释。集中度数字是下限——保守的名称匹配不会合并不同主体，但可能漏掉真正的重复。全政府口径的指标没有意义，所有数字都应在机构层面阅读。

Voucher Data Analysis Tool凭证数据分析工具

github.com/CHANGPeijia-ACC/voucher-analysis

Month-end voucher review, done in Python instead of by hand in Excel.

把月结前的凭证复核从 Excel 手工搬到 Python。

Problem
Pre-close voucher review is usually manual: filter for odd amounts, pivot by account, VLOOKUP against the bank statement. It is slow, and it is repeated every month.
月结前的凭证复核通常是手工的：筛异常金额、透视表做科目汇总、用 VLOOKUP 和银行流水核对。慢，而且每个月都要重来一遍。
Approach
Python standard library only — no third-party dependencies, runs with a single command. Two CSVs in (ledger and bank statement); out come validation results on screen and four CSV reports: monthly summary, month × account cross-tab, per-account outlier flags, and bank reconciliation.
仅用 Python 标准库，无第三方依赖，一条命令运行。输入账簿与银行流水两个 CSV，在屏幕上输出数据校验结果，并生成四份 CSV 报表：月度汇总、月份 × 科目交叉汇总、按科目的异常标记、银行对账。

Design decisions

Outlier thresholds are set per account rather than against a global mean — travel and office expenses sit at different magnitudes: in the sample data, three times the global mean is about 15,356 while three times the office-expense mean is 705, so a global threshold sets the bar for office items more than twenty times higher. Reconciliation is keyed on voucher number rather than row order, since real ledgers and bank statements differ in both count and sequence; keying also separates ledger-only from bank-only differences naturally. The multiple-of-mean check is skipped when an account's mean is non-positive, because "greater than 3x a negative number" would flag nearly everything.

异常阈值按科目分别设定，而非用全局均值——差旅费与办公费的量级完全不同：在示例数据中，全局均值的 3 倍约为 15,356，而办公费均值的 3 倍是 705，全局阈值会把办公费的异常门槛抬高二十多倍。对账以凭证号为键而非按行号顺序比对，因为真实账簿与银行流水在笔数和顺序上都不一致；以凭证号为键还能自然区分「只在账簿有」与「只在银行有」两类差异。当某科目均值非正时跳过倍数比较，否则「大于均值 3 倍」会退化成「大于一个更小的负数」，几乎所有记录都会被误判。
